# Booster Shop — 3D Product Module

**Edition:** 2026-08-29 v11.1 — FAQ value/ranking polish

## 1. Scope

Use for Booster Shop own 3D-printed goods. Do not apply this legal/composition layer to official sealed TCG products.

- `Тип виробу`;
- `Країна виготовлення`;
- `Спосіб виготовлення`;
- `Матеріал`;
- `Колір`;
- `Розміри`;
- `Маса`;
- `Комплектація`;
- `Вікове позиціонування` when the current catalog schema requires it;
- `Може трапитись у Mystery Box` — `Так/Ні`, never guess.

`Виробник = Booster Shop` is a native product field, not a duplicated attribute.

Current owner-confirmed material for the active 3D assortment: **`Пластик PLA`**. Change only when the owner explicitly changes the material rule for a product/series.

For keychains:

- `Матеріал фурнітури: метал`;
- exact ring/chain/carabiner type is not guaranteed and is not an attribute;
- mass marked `без фурнітури` when measured that way.

Use additional type-specific attributes only when meaningful and confirmed: movement, purpose, compatibility, display capacity, internal storage, etc.

## 3. Shopper-first body for 3D products

Core buyer-first and search-intent rules apply before manufacturing detail.

For a 3D product, body should answer what is interesting/useful about the **actual object**:

- keychain / small merch → carry/gift/visual appeal where confirmed;
- figure / fidget → interaction, pose, movement, mechanism or visual idea;
- display accessory → what it lets the collector show, organize, rotate, compare or store;
- kit/model format → what the buyer receives and what they need to do before the finished object exists.

Do not build body around geometry, slicer logic or a catalogue of dimensions. Technical facts belong in prose when they prove buyer value, clarify a meaningful limitation or answer a useful search intent.

### 3.1 First-party SEO advantage

Booster Shop physically produces/handles these goods, so prioritize **product-specific first-party information** over generic franchise filler.

When confirmed and useful, strong evidence includes:

- what moves and how the item can be posed/used;
- whether the item arrives assembled or requires assembly;
- actual size/weight when they help the shopper picture the object;
- material, fit, capacity or compatibility when decision-relevant;
- what is included;
- real differences between neighbouring models;
- practical production/finish details that affect expectations.

Search wording should emerge naturally from **what the product is and does**. Do not bolt generic Pokémon/One Piece lore onto a page merely to increase word count or keyword breadth.

## 4. Manufacturing as supporting evidence

In-house production may appear naturally where relevant, but it is supporting evidence/disclosure, not a mandatory plot point.

Possible confirmed realities:

- visible layer texture;
- minor support traces;
- small shade differences between production runs;
- exact metal fitting may differ from photo when hardware is not standardized.

Do not insert every caveat into every page.

Do not call natural layer texture a defect. Do not invent durability/heat-resistance claims.

Current owner-confirmed typical lead time when a 3D item is not ready in stock: **1–2 working days**.

## 5. IP-themed legal + contents FAQ

For IP-themed own 3D products, one FAQ item must carry all required meanings:

1. product is unofficial; Booster Shop is not a licensee, partner or affiliate of the rights holder;
2. exact package contents;
3. items in photos outside declared contents are not included unless stated otherwise.

Wording must be adapted to the concrete product; legal meaning cannot be weakened.

This disclosure FAQ serves a legal/content-clarification role and counts as one item inside the Core **2–4 FAQ flexible range**. **It does not count as proof that long-tail/search coverage is complete.** Add only the strongest additional product-specific questions; do not pad the FAQ merely to reach the upper end of the range.

For non-franchise 3D accessories, omit the licensing part and keep contents/photo clarification when relevant.

## 6. Other FAQ

Use Core FAQ rules.

Good 3D FAQ candidates arise from genuine product-specific uncertainty or search intent: mechanism, compatibility, capacity, assembly state, movement/static distinction, size interpretation, neighbouring model difference, production lead time, etc. These are candidate types, not a checklist.

Choose the strongest 2–4 items by value for the concrete product. Do not force every 3D card to the same FAQ structure, and do not use a FAQ slot to paraphrase a fact that body or attributes already explain sufficiently unless the answer adds a distinct practical clarification.

## 7. Prohibited IP claims / positioning language

Do not describe own IP-themed 3D products as:

`офіційний`, `ліцензійний`, `оригінальний товар Pokémon/One Piece`, `у співпраці з`, `сертифікований`, `іграшка`, `для дітей`, `гарантовано`, `100%`, `унікальний у світі`.

Do not introduce child-focused copy into product descriptions/FAQ unless the owner explicitly changes the catalog positioning.

## 8. 3D SKU naming mirror

Canonical source remains the current 3D SKU/naming convention; this module is only a working mirror.

Formula:

`<PREFIX>-<MNEMONIC>-<XYZ>`

Prefixes:

- `BR-` keychain/pendant;
- `FIG-` standalone figure;
- `ACC-3D-` functional 3D accessory.

Name formula:

`{Тип виробу} {Персонаж/Тема} ({Франшиза})[, {варіант}] — 3D-друк`

Do not infer a new subtype/naming rule from one product card; use the canonical naming source.

## 9. Dimension notation

Keep the approved axis canon:

- volumetric products → `Д×Ш×В`;
- flat products → `Ш×В×Т`.

Determine flat/volumetric from the product type and confirmed orientation, not by comparing the numerical dimensions.

Do not infer exact dimensions from photos. For current 3D work, use the owner-provided calculation/source table when it is available.
