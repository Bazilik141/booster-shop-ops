## Material design decision required

The approved handoff requires fixture stock to decrement both for sales and Track-2 write-offs.
The existing two fixture rows cannot do this safely today:

- recording usage in `Продажі!AE` is unsafe because the cost recomputation replaces that audit text;
- recording sales as `Списання` rows would misclassify ordinary sold-item inputs as write-offs and pollute
  existing write-off reporting;
- leaving `Розхідники!H` as a manually changed number breaks repeatability, idempotency, and low-stock
  warnings.

Recommended model: create a dedicated append-only CRM ledger, `Використання_фурнітури`, with one row per
fixture line (`id`, timestamp, source sale/write-off, fixture row/name, payer, quantity, frozen unit cost,
reference, note). The two fixture `H` formulas then sum that ledger; `Продажі!V/W` derives only from the
same ledger and the locked single-payer rule F9. This keeps consumption separate from human audit text and
from the `Списання` business meaning.

This is a material financial data-model choice, not a mechanical UI detail. Do not implement the form or
sale wiring until the owner approves this ledger, or explicitly chooses the weaker `Списання` reuse model.

avoids a new tab but makes normal product inputs look like write-offs in existing reporting.
