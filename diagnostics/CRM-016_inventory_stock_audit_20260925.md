## Confirmed findings

1. **The stock row mixes two ledgers.** `dashboard/booster-dashboard.html:2288-2340` loads `sku_list` and `stock_alerts` from the CRM, then `loadThreeDpStockOverlay_` overwrites only `row.stock` with the 3D-P `Наявно зараз, шт`. `physical_stock`, `reserved_total`, `projected_stock`, `projected_deficit`, `stock_status`, `action`, and `issues` remain based on CRM/automation. Thus `ACC-3D-PKM-110` can legitimately render 3D availability 2 alongside CRM-derived physical -1, deficit 1, and an automation recommendation to buy. These figures do not describe one consistent inventory state. The CRM `Склад!H` formula has no 3D manufacturing receipts. The Products tab at `dashboard/booster-dashboard.html:2858-2890` renders unoverlaid `sku_list`, so its 3D availability can also disagree with the Stock tab. The separate Alerts API at `crm/alerts-apps-script/Code.gs:158-190` also generates negative-stock alerts from automation's CRM balance, without a 3D-P check. The 3D-P API's default `3dp_skus` includes only active nomenclature; 11 of 73 current CRM 3D SKUs are inactive, so the dashboard does not overlay their 3D availability at all.

2. **Current CRM arithmetic is internally consistent, but that is not physical truth.** There are 190 unique `Склад` SKUs, exactly matched by 190 `Майстер_Товарів` and 190 `Черга_Складу` rows. Raw stock and incoming quantities agree across those three sheets for every SKU. For every SKU, `Склад!E/F/G/Q` agrees with the current 214 purchase, 410 sale, and 319 write-off source rows under the actual formula filters; `Склад!H` agrees with `E-F-G + migration in - migration out - preorder reservation` (with the Mystery Box exception), `Q-S=T`, and `S` agrees with open preorder sales. There were zero numeric residuals in these checks. These checks validate recorded transactions and formulas, not whether all real production or sales were recorded.

3. **Negative CRM available balances: 10 SKUs.** Four non-3D negatives are explained by open preorders: `OP-JP-OP07-BST` H=-1, reserved=1, CRM physical=0; `OP-JP-EB03-BST` H=-5, reserved=6, physical=1; `OP-JP-OP17-BST` H=-7, reserved=7, physical=0; `OP-JP-OP13-BST` H=-1, reserved=1, physical=0. `ACC-005` has H=-8 and no remaining open reservation after `OC-FOP-0396` changed from processing to shipped (19 received units, 26 qualifying sales including its 7, and 1 write-off). This is a real *accounting* shortage requiring transaction/physical-count review. The five 3D negatives are `BR-CHARM-100` -1, `BR-DITTO-400` -1, `ACC-3D-PKM-110` -1, `ACC-3D-PKM-200` -3, and `FIG-ONIX-500` -1. Their CRM balances omit printed stock, so they cannot be read as physical 3D shortages. `OC-FOP-0396` is currently shipped but unpaid; its 3 `ACC-3D-PKM-200` units have since been committed to 3D-P FIFO and CRM 3D accounting.

4. **Automation double-counts preorders in its projection.** For all 190 SKUs, `Черга_Складу` incoming equals `Склад!T = Q-S`. Its combined balance is `H+T`. But `H` has already subtracted `S`, so the queue's combined figure subtracts preorder demand twice. The currently affected SKUs are `OP-JP-OP07-BST` (queue 0 vs CRM projection 1), `OP-JP-EB03-BST` (-3 vs 3), `OP-JP-OP17-BST` (0 vs 7), and `OP-JP-OP13-BST` (33 vs 34). The CRM dashboard's quantities use `H+Q`, while the action/urgency inherited from the queue can use the lower figure. The four differences equal their preorder counts exactly. Separately, the queue currently recommends `Докупити` for five 3D SKUs (`BR-CHARM-100`, `BR-DITTO-400`, `ACC-3D-PKM-110`, `ACC-3D-PKM-200`, `FIG-ONIX-500`) because its stock basis is the uncorrected CRM balance.

5. **93 `Склад!H` rows use an older formula variant.** Every H cell is a formula, but 93 of 190 lack the migration and preorder terms present in the other 97. They currently produce the same numeric result because those 93 SKUs have no affected migration or preorder reservation; the structure is still a future defect. Example: `Склад!H115` for `ACC-3D-PKM-110` uses only `E-F-G`, while `H14` for `OP-JP-EB03-BST` includes migration and `-S`.

6. **A completed 3D sale failed to sync.** `Продажі!387` has received/paid `OC-FOP-0382`, `BR-DITTO-400`, quantity 1. There is no matching row in `3D_облік_замовлень`; the current 3D-P snapshot reports sold=0 and available=1 for this SKU, while CRM has one qualifying sale. `_Журнал_3DP_синхронізації` records five attempts on 2026-09-13 through 2026-09-15, all `skipped_api_error` with remote code `INTERNAL_ERROR`. Three other completed 3D sale rows (`ACC-3D-PKM-110`, `BR-CHARM-100`, `FIG-ONIX-500`) and the now-shipped `ACC-3D-PKM-200` row have matching 3D accounting rows. The 3D-P FIFO path in `3d-print/apps-script-3dp-api/CatalogFifo.gs:519` can fail before committing a sale; CRM deliberately logs only the remote error code in `crm/apps-script/Code.gs:2773`, so the journal alone cannot identify the underlying exception. Insufficient costed FIFO batches is one possible cause, not yet verified.

7. **One sale uses an orphaned historical SKU.** `Продажі!214` is a paid/received sale of 2 `PKM-JP-ABYSS-BST` units in `OC-FOP-0268`. `Склад` and automation contain only canonical `PKM-JP-ABYE-BST`. Thus that row is excluded from the canonical SKU's `Склад!F` sales total. `normalizeOpenCartSku_` in the CRM API maps this old code to the canonical one for future imports, but the stock formula compares existing Sheet text literally. All purchase/write-off/migration SKU references matched the current stock catalogue; this was the sole out-of-catalogue sales SKU. Verify whether another stock movement already compensated the two units before repairing history.

8. **3D-P sale timing is not defined by payment or receipt.** `sync3dpSalesV2_` commits a FIFO sale for any non-cancelled line passed to it; `apiUpdateSale_` calls it after an order edit without a paid/received status gate. During this audit, `OC-FOP-0396` changed from processing to shipped while still unpaid; the CRM journal then recorded `created` for its three `ACC-3D-PKM-200` units, and `3D_облік_замовлень` gained a matching accounting row. The current 3D-P JSON reports three sold units. Therefore 3D-P availability can already exclude an unpaid, unreceived order. Any cross-ledger projection must match individual CRM order lines to 3D-P allocations before subtracting reservations, or it can double-count demand again.

9. **Two catalogue/availability gaps remain.** The complete 3D-P result includes active `ACC-3D-TCG-600` with no availability object, while none of the 190 CRM/automation SKU rows has this code. `loadThreeDpStockOverlay_` inserts a bare Stock-tab row for it with `stock=0` and missing projection fields; the Products tab does not list it. `ACC-3D-DITTO-420` is inactive in CRM but active in 3D-P; `FIG-ONIX-201-GREY` exists in both catalogues, is archived, and has no 3D-P availability object. Missing availability must not silently mean zero. Confirm intended catalogue states before any write.

## Corrective design to review before implementation

- Make each displayed stock row use a single documented basis across Stock, Products, Overview, and stock alerts. For 3D SKUs, join 3D-P printed/defect/sold/bonus data to CRM lines and their 3D-P allocations. Define whether a 3D-P sale is recorded on reservation, shipment, or receipt, and derive availability, physical, forecast, deficit, status, and action together from that event contract. If the 3D-P read or reconciliation fails, show the 3D values as unavailable rather than silently retaining misleading CRM-derived physical/projection values. Include inactive SKUs when auditing all inventory.
- Reconcile every received/paid 3D CRM sale with its 3D-P accounting entry and FIFO allocation; surface failed syncs as actionable alerts. Diagnose `OC-FOP-0382` using a narrow 3D-P batch/API read or the corresponding Apps Script execution error before attempting a retry or repair.
- Use one preorder reservation exactly once in the automation queue's projected balance; then align its action/urgency with the quantities shown in the dashboard.
- Restore the canonical H formula across all stock rows through a controlled, owner-gated formula repair with baseline and post-repair integrity checks. Avoid manual literals in formula cells.
- Investigate `ACC-005` accounting shortage and the `PKM-JP-ABYSS-BST` historical alias against the underlying order and write-off records before changing live balances.
